## Two rules that bind us

**No identifiable people.** `testimonial.jpg` deliberately shows hands and a gowned
torso, never a face. The Pexels licence forbids implying that a person in a photo
endorses a product — and the testimonial quote is still sample copy. If a real
customer quote is added later, do **not** leave this photo beside it: a stock
photo next to a named attribution reads as a portrait of that person. Either use
a photo the customer supplies, or drop the image and let the quote stand alone.

**No unlicensed archival photography.** This project previously shipped
`jonas.jpg`, an archival photograph of Jonas Salk — a real, identifiable person,
almost certainly unlicensed. It was used as both the hero poster and the
testimonial portrait, and is now removed. Do not reintroduce a found image
without recording its licence here first.
